How it is verified

  • Systematic suite (local): 506 coarse sequences (box modes × handles × 4 strengths incl. extremes; warp pulls from every cell × 4 directions × 2 depths; anchors; 3 Perspective strengths × pulls short of / at / past the vanishing point; the pull-back class) and 150 seeded random walks, recorded on the reference and replayed through our UI, scored per shot on the picture's colour-region geometry (15 px), with reports and side-by-side strips. 2 of 11 coarse batches scored: 68 pass, 32 fail; random batch 1: 12 pass, 38 fail; the rest records overnight. Quick form: our replay against cached reference features, minutes, no VM. Not yet in the suite: warp presets and their values.
  • Render probe sets (earlier rounds): 48 reference sets for warp meshes and presets, scored pixel by pixel; impulse probes for the resamplers.
  • Randomized self-checks (ours only): 12,000 walks for flips, stalls and non-proposed landings.
  • Unit tests pinned to the reference's saved states: give-way, stored maps, pulls, the drag's grid, the surface solver.
