  4. Box edge rules 2026-11-21
    Edge drags of skewed boxes and top/bottom edges in perspective match (craft-libs #2308); Alt/Shift laws fitted to ~1 px.
  5. Give-way first fit 2026-11-22
    The placement's give-way on a pull toward the viewer fitted at one strength; the fly-off after a pull past the vanishing point reported.
  6. Fly-off solved (four causes) 2026-11-23
    Give-way decoded from 88 saved reference files; the pull taken through the drawn map; the drag's grid kept; the surface solver on meshes spanning 1e8 px. Verified on the hand recipe step by step.
  7. No turn-around; one undo per drag 2026-11-23
    The near edge runs on to the horizon as the reference's does (600 px pull-back matches to ~30 px, both leave the canvas at ~500 px). A drag with pauses is one undo step.
  8. Wireframe look 2026-11-23
    Stems from corners to handle points, anchors as small dots, dotted crossed-frame diagonals, the values readout above the pointer (craft-libs, local).
  9. Systematic suite 2026-11-23
    506 coarse sequences and 150 random walks recorded on the reference and replayed through our UI, scored per shot; first batches: Perspective cross-over, far Skew, auto-scroll, the floating bar in the rotate zone.
  10. XOR wireframe 2026-11-23
    Overlays draw as each channel XOR 128 over the frame, as the reference does: backdrop callbacks in the egui fork, an XOR painter in craft-libs; verified pixel for pixel (values, rows and columns, 5 px handles) on the undo and wire-look probes. Undo inside a transform verified against the reference: one step per drag.
  11. Whole-pixel canvas at zoom 2026-11-24
    The 1/3-zoom residual was the reference's canvas extent: ceil(size × zoom) device pixels per axis with the pointer mapped over them. Implemented as a per-axis stretch on the framework's view (zoom stays nominal). Coarse 406 of 506.
  12. Off-mesh presses and concave Distort 2026-11-24
    A press off the drawn warp mesh pulls nothing and a point's reach is the measured 11.75 px; a Distort corner past the diagonal shows the source and commits nothing. Coarse 424 of 506.
  13. View scroll at the window's edge 2026-11-25
    The p0/p1 'fly-off' frames were the reference scrolling its view under a drag past the window's edge, not the give-way. Implemented per pointer move; coarse 453 of 506, random 93 of 149.
  14. Crossed and concave boxes 2026-11-25
    A box dragged over itself keeps its outline and draws the picture as the rational bilinear patch through its corners (93–98 % pixel agreement).
  15. Box press rules 2026-11-25
    Side bands where the view reads the pointer, Perspective sides slide, Perspective ignores presses outside the box, corner reach, and no view scroll for a drag that grabbed nothing.
  16. Edge scroll bound 2026-11-25
    A drag past the window's edge stops scrolling with 100 px of the canvas in view; Warp surface pulls scroll half steps. Evening build: coarse 444 of 506, random 110 of 150 (morning build 409 / 63, same scorer).
  17. Window layout and corner zone 2026-11-26
    Scroll bars sit beside the view as in the reference, so the canvas is centred the same way at every zoom; a press just outside a box corner goes to the side the reference gives it.
  18. Warp after Perspective, far press, pull-backs 2026-11-26
    A warp pull past a perspective's horizon grabs, shares and gives way as the reference's does; a press far outside the box applies the transform; pull-backs follow. Coarse 409 → 490 of 506 since the day before, measured the same way.
  19. Warp presets as people use them 2026-11-27
    Switching presets keeps H/V and orientation, presets stay editable on turned and perspective boxes, scrubbable Bend/H/V, the preset's handle, the 'discards your edits' question. A new set of 96 recorded preset sequences: 43 → 83 match every step.

    Files the reference saved with a warp on a smart object no longer come apart at the next edit, and presets are saved and reopened by name. Re-opening 194 reference files: 131 far off before, 9 after.
  21. Free Transform by the numbers 2026-11-27

  22. Warp Text 2026-11-28
